🏠 Family Pantry · 1-Click Google Sheets Setup

Zero coding needed — just click the Copy buttons below and paste into your Family Food Rations & Pantry Tracker sheet (~2 minutes total).

1

Open Apps Script in Your Google Sheet

  1. Go to your Family Food Rations & Pantry Tracker tab (we just opened it in your browser!).
  2. In the top menu, click Extensions → Apps Script.
Open Google Sheet ↗
2

Copy & Paste Code.gs

Copied ✓
  1. Click the green button below to copy Code.gs.
  2. In Apps Script, click inside Code.gs, press Cmd + A then Delete, then Cmd + V to paste.
  3. Press Cmd + S (or click the Save icon).
v2.3 · 2,384 lines ready (Table-safe + Auto-Sort)
3

Create Index.html & Paste

Copied ✓
  1. On the left side of Apps Script next to Files, click + → choose HTML.
  2. Type Index (capital I, exact spelling) and press Enter.
  3. Click the green button below to copy Index.html.
  4. Select everything inside Index.html (Cmd + A → Delete), paste (Cmd + V), and press Cmd + S.
v2.0 Modern Single-Tap · 5,033 lines ready
4

Publish & Pin to Your Wife's Android Phone

  1. In Apps Script (top-right), click blue Deploy → New deployment.
  2. Click the gear icon (⚙️) next to Select type → choose Web app.
  3. Set Description: Family Pantry App  |  Execute as: Me  |  Who has access: Anyone.
  4. Click Deploy → Authorize access → pick your Gmail.
  5. Copy your new Web app URL and text it to your wife. On her Android phone, open it in Chrome → tap ⋮ (3 dots) → Add to Home screen → name it Family Pantry!
Normal one-time Google screen: If Google says "Google hasn't verified this app", that always shows for personal scripts you own. Just click Advanced → Go to [Project Name] (unsafe) → Allow.
